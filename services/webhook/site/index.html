How it works

Three steps, and there is no TikTok login in any of them.

1

Get Stash

One payment on the App Store, then sign in with Apple. Stash asks Apple for no name and no email address — only an anonymous identifier.

2

Send it videos

Tap Share on any TikTok and pick Stash to save it there and then. For everything you saved before, ask TikTok for your data export and import the file.

3

The library builds itself

Stash reads the caption, listens to the audio and reads the text on screen, then writes each video up and files it by what it is.

What Stash makes for you

Each saved video becomes something you can actually use, sorted by what it is.

Recipe cards

Cooking videos become clean recipe cards with the ingredients and the steps, ready when you are.

Track list

Music you saved becomes a track list with title and artist, and a link out to play it — including releases named out loud but never written in the caption.

How-to summaries

Coding and how-to videos become short summaries with the links and tools they mention.

Search instead of scroll

Everything gets a title, a summary and topics, so you can find the one you half-remember.

Where your data actually goes

This does not all happen on your phone. Our servers download each video you saved from TikTok and delete the file as soon as they are done with it — no copies are kept. The audio goes to Groq for speech-to-text, and the caption, transcript and on-screen text go to AWS Bedrock in Frankfurt to produce the title, summary and category. If you import an export, the rest of it — messages, watch history, profile, wallet — is ignored on your phone and never reaches us. The privacy policy names every provider, region and retention period.

What Stash does and does not do

The limits, stated plainly, so you know before you pay.

One payment, no subscription

Each account can process 500 videos to begin with, then 100 more each month. Transcribing and analysing a video costs real money, so the allowance is a real limit rather than a sales tactic.

iPhone only

iOS 17 or later. There is no iPad app, no Android app and no web version.

No TikTok account connection

Stash never asks for your TikTok password, cannot read your account and cannot post to it. Everything it knows comes from a video you shared into it or a data export you requested yourself.

You can take it all back

Settings has Export my data and Delete account. Deleting removes your library from our servers and revokes the Sign in with Apple grant.
