A searchable library, built from your own TikTok export

You ask TikTok for your data, hand the file to Stash, and the videos you saved to Favourites come back as recipe cards, a track list and how-to summaries you can search.

How it works

Three steps, and there is no TikTok login in any of them.

1

Join with an invite

Stash is an invite-only beta for iPhone. You sign in with Apple and enter your code — Stash asks Apple for no name and no email address.

2

Ask TikTok for your data

In TikTok's own settings: Account → Download your data, in JSON format. TikTok prepares the file and you download it yourself.

3

Import the file

Pick it in Stash. Stash reads only the Favourite Videos entries in it and builds your library from those.

What Stash makes for you

Each saved video becomes something you can actually use, sorted by what it is.

Recipe cards

Cooking videos become clean recipe cards with the ingredients and the steps, ready when you are.

Track list

Music you saved becomes a track list with title and artist, and a link out to play it.

How-to summaries

Coding and how-to videos become short summaries with the links and tools they mention.

Search instead of scroll

Everything gets a title, a summary and topics, so you can find the one you half-remember.

Where your data actually goes

This does not all happen on your phone. Our servers download each video you saved from TikTok and delete the file as soon as they are done with it — no copies are kept. The audio goes to Groq for speech-to-text, and the caption, transcript and on-screen text go to AWS Bedrock in Frankfurt to produce the title, summary and category. The rest of your export — messages, watch history, profile, wallet — is ignored on your phone and never reaches us. The privacy policy names every provider, region and retention period.

Where this stands today

Stash is early software run by one person. These are the limits, not a roadmap.

Invite-only, and free

The beta is capacity-limited: you need an invite code. Each account can import 500 videos, then 100 more per month, because every import costs real money to transcribe and analyse.

iPhone only

iOS 17 or later, distributed through TestFlight. There is no iPad app, no Android app and no web version.

No TikTok account connection

Stash cannot read your TikTok account or post to it, and never asks for your TikTok password. We applied for TikTok's Data Portability API on 11 July 2026 and it is still in review; until then, the export you request yourself is the only way in.

You can take it all back

Settings has Export my data and Delete account. Deleting removes your library from our servers and revokes the Sign in with Apple grant.
